glossary

four defined terms — the ids are what the report links to

Free cash flow
Cash from operations less capital expenditure. Reported before acquisitions, which is why it flatters an acquisitive company.
WACC
Weighted average cost of capital — the discount rate a DCF applies to future cash flows. A one-point change moves this valuation by roughly 14%.
Net working capital
Receivables plus inventory less payables. A rise consumes cash even when it accompanies growth.
Total shareholder return
Share price change plus dividends, expressed as a compound annual rate over the stated period.
