Competitive position

Rivalry High

Two credible competitors at 3x and 0.4x the revenue, both funding a full roadmap

Supplier power Very high

One foundry at the leading node; allocation is negotiated annually and not contractually secured

Buyer power Moderate

Top five customers are 38% of revenue, but switching costs a design cycle of 18 months

Substitutes Low

No general-purpose alternative at this performance per watt; ASICs address narrow workloads only

New entrants Low

$9bn of annual R&D and a decade of IP; three well-funded attempts have exited since 2015

Supplier power is the binding constraint; everything else is favourable.
